Lineups for every competitive map

CS2 nades, smokes & tricks in your pocket.

Key Features

Built for competitive CS2

Everything you need to learn smokes, flashes, molotovs, and map tricks — native Swift, dark UI, no account required.

🗺️

Minimap lineups

Browse grenade clusters on every competitive map. Tap a yellow landing spot, then a green throw position.

💥

Nades, smokes & more

Filter by smoke, flash, molotov, or HE. Switch CT, T, or any team. Same flow for map tricks and boosts.

🖼️

Step-by-step images

Open lineup photos with a crosshair overlay. Pinch-zoom the gallery. Share a YouTube link or image URL.

▶️

Optional video demos

When a creator recorded a throw, watch it in an embedded YouTube player. No autoplay. Skip Video to stay off Google.

✨

Latest feed

Newest nades and tricks first. Search by title or map. Open a card for the same detail gallery as the minimap.

🔒

No account, no ads

Filters stay on device. No analytics SDK. No tracking. Lineups come from staff-approved posts on cs2tricks.com.
