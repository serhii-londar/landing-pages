Privacy Policy

Last updated: 19 September 2026

CS2 Tricks ("the App") is a companion for browsing Counter-Strike 2 grenade lineups and tricks on iOS and iPadOS. This policy describes how data is handled when you use the app.

1. Data we collect

The app does not create accounts, does not include analytics or crash reporters, and does not sell data.

2. Data stored locally on your device

  • Filters and last tab: Map, nade type, team, category, and the last selected tab are stored on device with Apple’s UserDefaults so they survive relaunch. That data never leaves the device through us.

3. Network requests & third parties

Lineup content is loaded from api.cs2tricks.com and images from cs2tricks.com. Those requests include a normal network path (IP address, user agent) that any HTTPS client sends. We do not attach an advertising ID.

Some lineups include an optional video. Playback uses an embedded YouTube player (youtube-nocookie.com). If you open Video, Google/YouTube may collect data under YouTube’s privacy policy. We do not receive that data. Skip Video to avoid the embed.

Share sends a YouTube link or an image URL through Apple’s share sheet. We do not log what you share.

4. Children’s privacy

The app is not directed at children under the age of 13.

5. Changes

Updates to this policy will be reflected with a revised "Last updated" date at the top of this document.
