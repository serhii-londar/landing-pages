# Privacy Policy

Last updated: 20 September 2026

This iOS app is unofficial and independently developed. It is not the official CS2 Tricks app. Lineup content is loaded from `cs2tricks.com`, a third-party site we do not operate.

This policy describes what the iOS app does with information.

## Data we collect

The app does **not** create accounts, does not include analytics or crash reporters, and does not sell data. Filter choices (map, nade type, team, category) and the last selected tab are stored **on device** with Apple’s UserDefaults so they survive relaunch. That data never leaves the device through us.

Lineup content is loaded from `api.cs2tricks.com` and images from `cs2tricks.com`. Those requests include a normal network path (IP address, user agent) that any HTTPS client sends. We do not attach an advertising ID.

## YouTube

Some lineups include an optional video. Playback uses an embedded YouTube player (`youtube-nocookie.com`). If you open Video, Google/YouTube may collect data under [YouTube’s privacy policy](https://www.youtube.com/howyoutubeworks/user-settings/privacy/). We do not receive that data. Skip Video to avoid the embed.

## Sharing

Share sends a YouTube link or an image URL through Apple’s share sheet. We do not log what you share.

## Children

The app is not directed at children under 13.
