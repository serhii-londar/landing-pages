Frequently Asked Questions

How often is hero, item, and patch data updated?

Dota 2 Wiki dynamically fetches the latest hero attributes, facet trees, ability stats, and balance changelogs directly from Valve's official public game endpoints. Whenever Valve releases a new balance patch (e.g. 7.37, 7.38) or hero update, fresh data is automatically synchronized upon opening the app.

Does Dota 2 Wiki work without an internet connection?

Yes! All hero details, item recipes, and patch note entries are automatically cached locally on your device storage. If you lose internet access, the app seamlessly serves cached data so you can continue reading abilities, stats, and builds offline.

How do I change the app language?

You can change the language at any time by tapping the Language selector icon (🌐) in the top-right corner of the navigation bar. The app supports English, Ukrainian, Spanish, Chinese, Russian, Portuguese, German, and more.

Are hero facets, innate abilities, and Aghanim's upgrades included?

Yes. Every hero breakdown includes their innate abilities, available Facet modifiers, Aghanim's Scepter upgrades, Aghanim's Shard enhancements, and the complete 10/15/20/25 level talent tree.

What devices and iOS versions are supported?

Dota 2 Wiki is designed and optimized for:

  • iPhone: iOS 16.0 or later (including iPhone 14 Pro, 15, 16 Pro Dynamic Island and full-bleed displays)
  • iPad: iPadOS 16.0 or later with multi-column layout support
  • Mac: macOS 13.0 or later on Apple Silicon (M1/M2/M3/M4) Macs
Does the app collect or track my personal data?

No. We respect your privacy. Dota 2 Wiki does not require account creation, does not use tracking cookies, and does not include third-party advertising SDKs or data brokers. For full details, please check our Privacy Policy.

Is Dota 2 Wiki affiliated with or sponsored by Valve Corporation?

No. Dota 2 Wiki is an independent, fan-created reference application. Dota 2 and all related trademarks, logos, and character designs are the property of Valve Corporation.
