Frequently Asked Questions

What is DevTo for iOS?

DevTo is a fast, native iOS and iPadOS client designed for reading and exploring software engineering articles published on DEV Community (dev.to). It offers distraction-free reading, code syntax highlighting, custom tag filtering, and offline bookmarking.

Do I need an account to use the app?

No. You can browse trending articles, search topics, filter by tags, and save bookmarks locally without creating an account or logging in.

Does DevTo support offline reading?

Yes! You can bookmark any article to your local Reading List with a single tap. Saved articles and their cached contents can be read offline without an active internet connection.

Which devices and iOS versions are supported?

DevTo is optimized for:

  • iPhone: iOS 15.0 or later (with full ProMotion 120Hz display support)
  • iPad: iPadOS 15.0 or later with multi-column sidebar navigation
  • Mac: macOS 12.0 or later on Apple Silicon (M1/M2/M3/M4) Macs
Does the app collect or track my personal data?

The app does not collect personal identity information. Diagnostics data and crash telemetry are handled securely via Firebase Crashlytics to improve stability. For full details, please review our Privacy Policy.

Is this an official DEV Community app?

DevTo is an independent, native client built by Serhii Londar utilizing the public DEV Community (Forem) API. DEV and DEV Community are trademarks of Forem Inc.
